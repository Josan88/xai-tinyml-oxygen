Information for participants

Study: User evaluation of an explainable oxygen-risk dashboard.

Researcher: Jason Yong Sheng Ng, Bachelor of Engineering (Honours) (Software), Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak. Supervisor: Dr Sheena Punai Philimon.

What is this about?

The dashboard shows a score for short recordings from a finger pulse sensor (10-second PPG windows). The score is how likely a window is Low-Oxygen Risk. It also shows which signal features moved the score. We want to know whether people read the screen correctly and whether the explanation is useful.

What will I do?

You will look at 4 recorded windows and answer a few questions about each, then two short questionnaires. It takes about 10 minutes. The windows come from a public research dataset (OpenOximetry). No sensor is used and none of your own body data is involved.

What is collected?

Your answers and how long each part took. We do not collect your name, email, IP address, or any health data. Answers cannot be linked to you, so once you press Submit they cannot be withdrawn.

Do I have to take part?

No. It is voluntary. You can stop at any time by closing the page. Nothing is saved until you press Submit on the last page.

How will results be used?

Anonymous, combined results will appear in the final-year project report, paper, and presentation. Data are kept on the researcher's computer in line with Swinburne research data rules.

This prototype is not a medical device and does not give a diagnosis.
